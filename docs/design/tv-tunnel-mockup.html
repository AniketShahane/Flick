The moment your film lands on the TV

Five new takes on Arrival, plus the original and the earlier directions. Each preview plays one whole cast: the room at rest, your swipe, the wait while the TV connects, and the first frame. The strip under each one shows what your phone is doing at that same moment.

The Arrival family

B

Arrival

The one you liked

The phone's hyperspace carries on onto the TV, flying outward toward you. It uses the same rings, streaks and blue wash. On the first frame both screens flash white together.

Reveal
White flash, in step with the phone
TV cost
Medium. About 16 moving strokes a frame
B1

Drop Out

The TV sits deep in the warp, with the stars stretched into long streaks. On the first frame it drops out of hyperspace: the streaks snap back into stars, slow down and fade away, and the film settles in behind them.

Reveal
Braking out of the warp, no flash
TV cost
Medium. 24 streaks while waiting
B2

Tunnel

Your pick

TV-shaped rings rush past you, forming a tunnel with a glowing window at its far end. On the first frame the real film starts playing in that window as it draws nearer. A beat later it flies down the tunnel to fill the screen, as if it travelled all the way from your phone.

Reveal
The film itself arrives, already playing
TV cost
Medium. 7 rings, one clipped film
B3

Card Ride

The blue Flick card rides the warp toward you from deep in the field, growing as it comes. On the first frame it speeds up, fills the screen and turns into the film as the streaks whip past the edges.

Reveal
The card becomes the film
TV cost
Medium. The current card, plus the warp
B4

Shockwave

The warp runs in reverse. Streaks pour into the centre and build a bright amber-and-blue core while the TV connects. On the first frame the core bursts, and a ring of light races out to the corners with the film behind it.

Reveal
An expanding ring wipes the film in
TV cost
Medium. One clip and a glow
B5

Vortex

The warp twists into a slow spiral, like water circling a drain. On the first frame the spiral spins up, then flashes white with the phone and opens onto the film.

Reveal
Spin-up, then the shared flash
TV cost
Medium. Same strokes as Arrival
